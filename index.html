How the DFAs are defined (for viva)

Email DFA M = (Q, Σ, δ, q0, F)

  • Q = {q0, q1, …, q18, qTRAP}
  • Σ = letters, digits and . _ - @
  • q0 = start, q1 = inside the name part, q2 = just after @, q3…q18 = matching each letter of ashokacollege.in
  • F = {q18}. Any wrong or extra character goes to qTRAP and never comes back.

Password DFA

  • A state is written q<UL DS>,n: four bits say whether an uppercase, lowercase, digit and symbol have been seen, and n is the length so far (capped at 8).
  • That gives 16 × 9 = 144 states. F = {q1111,8}.
